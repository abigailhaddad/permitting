Federal jobs that mention “permitting”

Every USAJOBS announcement from 2017 to now whose full text contains the word “permitting,” combining two sources: a scraped corpus of ~2M announcements (2017–March 2026) and a live-API archive that extends it to the present. A companion dataset in the repo (expanded_jobs.csv, ~50k announcements) also covers other permitting-related terms that jobs use when they skip the word. Hover a mention type to see which specific words drove the call; click it to read the actual text around each mention. All rules live in patterns.yaml. Links open the announcement on USAJOBS (closed jobs still display, marked as closed).
